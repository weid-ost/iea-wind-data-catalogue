Task 52 – Citing the lidar ontology

Publication Title: OpenOA. Publication Type: Software. Date of publication: May 2024. Please cite the release, however you prefer to write it:

  • Bare: 10.5281/zenodo.11289823
  • Prefixed: doi:10.5281/ZENODO.11289823
  • Resolver: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11289823
  • Legacy resolver: http://dx.doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11289823

All four are the same record. Older citations sometimes use the info:doi/10.5281/zenodo.11289823 form as well.
