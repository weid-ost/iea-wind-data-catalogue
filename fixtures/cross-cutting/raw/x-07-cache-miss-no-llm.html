Task 59 — closing note

The operating agent has circulated a closing note to participants. It summarises where the work reached, what remains open, and how the material will be looked after once the extension period ends. Copies were sent to the national representatives on the eleventh, and to the wider participant list a week later.

Several participants asked whether the intermediate material would stay reachable. It will: the operating agent has undertaken to keep the existing landing pages in place, unchanged, and to redirect rather than remove anything that later moves. Nothing is being withdrawn.

Questions about the closing note itself should go to the operating agent in the first instance, and to the executive committee secretariat if a formal answer is needed.
