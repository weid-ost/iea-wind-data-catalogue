Task 43 – Selected publications

A short reading list from Working Group 1, written as running prose rather than as a table.

  • Clark, Thomas (2023) “Powerful Power Curves: A Production-Proven, Open-Source Schema for Wind Turbine Power Curves”, poster presented at Wind Europe 2023, doi:10.5281/zenodo.7940068. The schema is in production use.
  • Marykovskiy, Y. et al. (2024) “Knowledge engineering for wind energy”, Wind Energy Science 9, 883–917, 10.5194/wes-9-883-2024.
  • See also the WES special issue introduction at https://doi.org/10.5194/wes-8-947-2023, which sets out the three Grand Challenges (Clifton et al., 2023).

Publication Title, Authors and Date of publication for the full list are maintained on the working group pages.
